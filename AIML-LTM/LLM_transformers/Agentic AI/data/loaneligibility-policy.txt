Bank Loan Eligibility & Policy Knowledge Base
1. Overview

This document defines the internal lending policies used for automated loan eligibility screening.
The AI system retrieves these rules when evaluating customer loan requests based on:

Salary / Monthly Income

Credit Score

Existing EMI obligations

Loan type requested

Loan tenure

Loan amount requested

The goal is to determine:

Loan eligibility

Maximum loan amount

Suitable loan product

Recommended tenure and EMI structure

2. Common Eligibility Rules
2.1 Minimum Age

Minimum age: 21 years

Maximum age at loan maturity: 65 years

2.2 Credit Score Requirements
Credit Score Range	Risk Category	Lending Decision
750+	Excellent	Full eligibility
700 – 749	Good	Eligible with standard interest
650 – 699	Moderate	Eligible with higher interest
600 – 649	High Risk	Restricted loan amount
Below 600	Very High Risk	Usually rejected
2.3 EMI Burden Ratio Rule

Total EMI obligations should not exceed:

50% of monthly income

Formula:

Total EMI Ratio = (Existing EMIs + Proposed EMI) / Monthly Income

Decision rules:

EMI Ratio	Decision
<= 40%	Safe lending
41–50%	Moderate risk
>50%	Loan not recommended
